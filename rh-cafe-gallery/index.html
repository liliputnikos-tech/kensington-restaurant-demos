R&H Cafe Gallery

Persian dishes made from scratch, homemade cakes and artisan coffee, served among contemporary Iranian art.

Wednesday to Sunday, 9am – 5pm

Statement

Come for the flavour, stay for the art.

R&H began on Richmond Hill in 2014 and has traded since 2016, bringing together traditional Persian cooking, Middle Eastern-inspired bites, homemade cakes and expertly brewed coffee in a gallery setting. Its home is now a residential corner of Kensington, W8.

Breakfast is the heart of it: eggs, dates, figs, pistachios and house jams with Persian flatbread and San Francisco sourdough. There is no service charge. On busy days, tables are held for an hour after food is served.
