Ingredients

Imported from Italy, made on Bute Street

Pugliese burrata, Calabrese salami and speciality artichokes, all imported from Italy. Everything else is made fresh on the premises, every day.

Café · bar

Espresso at 7.30,
a spritz by evening.

Caffè

Espresso, cappuccino, latte, mocha, Americano, ginseng, babyccino. Oat, soya, almond and lactose-free milk.

Tè

English breakfast, green, chamomile, Earl Grey, lemon & ginger, mint, jasmine, fennel & liquorice and more.

Aperitivo

Peroni, Ichnusa from Sardinia, Prosecco, wines by the glass, Spritz, Negroni. Happy hour every Friday from 6.30pm.

Analcolici

San Pellegrino Limonata, Chinotto and Aranciata, freshly squeezed orange juice, Acqua Panna.
