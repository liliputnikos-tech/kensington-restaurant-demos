The name

میکده meykade: in Persian, a house of wine.

A small, warm room on Fulham Road, a few minutes from South Kensington. Meykade brings the hospitality of a Persian gathering to Chelsea: slow mornings over tea and cake, then glasses of natural wine as the evening draws in.
